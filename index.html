How the model reads a sentence

Every word becomes a vector, then two stacked recurrent layers read the sentence forwards and backwards so each word is judged in context. The last layer picks one of nine tags per word.

120

Words in

Sentences are cut or padded to 120 tokens.

128

Embedding

Each word maps to a 128-number vector. Unknown words share one slot.

64

BiLSTM

Reads left-to-right and right-to-left, with dropout of 0.3.

32

BiLSTM

A second pass refines the context, again with dropout of 0.3.

9

Softmax

One tag per word: O plus B- and I- for four entity types.
